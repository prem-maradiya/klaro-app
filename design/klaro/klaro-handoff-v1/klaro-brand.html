04 — Usage

Do & don't

Do

  • Keep the gradient light corner top-left, blue bottom-right.
  • Use the flat mark on light or photographic backgrounds.
  • Give the mark ≥ 1× stem-width of clear space.
  • Swap to the flat K at favicon sizes (≤32px).

    • Don't

  • Recolor, rotate, or stretch the K.
  • Put the dark-fold version on light backgrounds.
  • Add outlines or extra drop shadows.
  • Place the mark on busy imagery without a tile.
